Merian sky viewer

Browse the Merian images in the Legacy Surveys sky viewer. The public viewer is open to everyone. Its layer menu has the Merian DR1 N540 and N708 images, in color or on their own, next to the HSC and Legacy Surveys images of the same sky. The internal viewer is for members of the Merian collaboration and asks for the collaboration login. Contact Yifei Luo (yifeiluo@lbl.gov) for the viewer credentials.
