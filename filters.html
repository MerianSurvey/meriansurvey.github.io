The Merian filters

Merian observes with two medium-band filters built for the Dark Energy Camera, N540 and N708. Together with the five HSC-SSP broad bands they give seven-band photometry.

Design

N540 and N708

N708 was designed to catch Hα and N540 to catch [O III] from the same galaxies. The design and the laboratory tests of both filters are described in Luo et al. (2024).

Transmission curves

The mean laboratory transmission curves of the two Merian filters are available as CSV files, with wavelength in nm and transmission from 0 to 1. They describe the filters alone, without the telescope, the camera, the detector or the atmosphere.

  • N540 transmissionCSV, 0.2 nm steps
  • N708 transmissionCSV, 0.2 nm steps

The HSC team distributes response curves for the HSC filters. Those include the atmosphere, the telescope, the camera optics and the detector as well as the filters.

Across the filter

Shifts with position

The transmission curves move slightly with distance from the filter center. From the center to 280 mm, close to the edge of the 600 mm clear aperture, N540 shifts by about 1.1 nm to the blue and N708 by about 1.8 nm to the red.
