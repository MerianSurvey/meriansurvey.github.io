Data

Data releases

Merian provides seven-band photometry, HSC grizy plus N540 and N708, for sources across the survey footprint.

Data Release 1

DR1

The first data release is described in Danieli et al. (2025). It is built from observations taken between March 2021 and March 2022.

Instructions for accessing DR1 images and catalogs will be posted here.
Full color
320 deg² observed in both N540 and N708
Full depth
234 deg² at full depth in both filters
Bands
N540, N708 and HSC-SSP g, r, i, z, y
Photometry
Aperture-matched GAaP photometry for accurate colors
Pipeline
LSST Science Pipelines, on the HSC-SSP pixel grid

Looking ahead

DR2

The final data release will cover the entire survey area, about 733 deg² in both filters and about 584 deg² at full depth, processed with a more recent version of the LSST Science Pipelines.

Acknowledging Merian

How to cite

If you use Merian data, please cite the two survey papers, Danieli et al. (2025) for the survey and its first data release and Luo et al. (2024) for the filter design. Both are listed on the publications page.
